# B4.16 Paper Order Lifecycle Contract

**Status:** Implemented and verified locally on 2026-10-28 for the synthetic, paper-only broker.

## Decision

The local B4 lifecycle remains deliberately small:

- Orders receive full fills only. Synthetic market-event `volume` is informational and is not executable liquidity.
- Eligible orders are processed in accepted ledger order. Acceptance order is the tie-breaker even when submitted timestamps differ.
- Market buys use the synthetic ask and sells use the synthetic bid. Limit buys require `ask <= limit_price`; limit sells require `bid >= limit_price`, with the existing adverse-slippage checks preserved.
- An unmatched limit remains open across later event time and retains its derived reservation until it fills, is cancelled, or reaches a terminal risk outcome.
- Cancellation is an idempotent command. Terminal fill, cancellation, and risk rejection release derived reservations.
- Order expiry is disabled. Approval expiry remains a separate command-approval rule and does not expire open orders.
- A market-event failure restores the complete pre-event snapshot; no partial event effect is committed.

## Recovery and compatibility

The existing `market-os.b4-1.paper-portfolio-event@v1`, order, fill, snapshot, checkpoint, and fingerprint projections remain unchanged. Recovery restores verified resulting snapshots and validates their fingerprints; it does not independently reinterpret event volume or claim semantic event reduction.

## Deferred expansion

Partial fills or expiry require a new versioned contract defining executable liquidity, residual quantity, fee and minimum-notional behavior, reservation release, event-time deadlines, cancellation of residuals, and recovery invariants. No such lifecycle expansion is enabled by B4.16.

The implementation remains local, synthetic, paper-only, and Python-only. CLI and HTTP mutation routes, providers, credentials, network access, live venues, and production infrastructure remain out of scope.
