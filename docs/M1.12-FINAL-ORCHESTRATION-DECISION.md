# M1.12: Final orchestration decision

**Date:** 2026-10-14
**Status:** Complete
**Decision:** **Accepted — Market OS owns orchestration; OpenHarness is optional**

## Decision summary

Market OS owns the production workflow, safety policies, event trace,
persistence, cancellation, restart recovery, multi-agent aggregation, and
deployment boundary. OpenHarness remains an optional adapter for agent-runtime
capabilities. It is not required to install or run the dependency-free core.

This closes the repeated provisional cycle. M2 can start at the root-owned
contract seam without waiting for OpenHarness-native recovery or deployment
features.

## Why this is final

Market OS already provides and tests:

- `MarketWorkflow` as the single production workflow;
- paper-only task and order contracts;
- cancellation checkpoints and terminalization;
- restart cleanup with inspectable recovery metadata;
- idempotent results and content-addressed artifact lineage;
- ordered multi-agent aggregation with duplicate and conflict records;
- bounded local service lifecycle, request-body timeout, and structured logs.

M1.11 also records the remaining OpenHarness-native and deployment gaps:
synchronous persistence cancellation, checkpoint/resume, teammate propagation,
duplicate handling, provider execution deadlines, process quotas, and service
identity/filesystem permissions. These gaps are not safe reasons to transfer
ownership to OpenHarness.

## Responsibility map

See [ADR-0010](decisions/ADR-0010-final-orchestration-owner.md) for the
normative ownership table. The practical rule is simple: adapters may provide
capabilities, but Market OS owns the run state and the safety decision.

## M2 readiness

M2 is unblocked for planning and deterministic implementation. It remains
blocked from real providers, credentials, brokers, live execution, and broad
runtime integration until those are separately approved. The first adapter is
the narrow offline Vibe-Trading market-data/backtest seam described in
[M2-RESEARCH-ADAPTER-PLAN.md](M2-RESEARCH-ADAPTER-PLAN.md).

## Historical record

ADR-0002 and ADR-0004 through ADR-0009 remain unchanged. They capture the
provisional proposals, runtime probes, blocker classifications, and wrapper
decisions that led to this final owner selection.
