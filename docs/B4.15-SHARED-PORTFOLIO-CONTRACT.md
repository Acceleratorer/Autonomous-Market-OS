# B4.15 Shared Portfolio Contract

**Status:** Implemented and verified locally on 2026-10-28 for the synthetic, paper-only Python broker.

## Boundary

B4.15 adds shared membership for one local BTCUSDT Spot, long-only paper portfolio. It does not add an HTTP or CLI mutation route, a provider, credentials, network access, live trading, deployment, or distributed ownership.

## Identity and membership

- A shared portfolio is created with `B41Genesis.create_shared(shared_portfolio_id, fixture_lineage, member_strategy_ids, policy)`.
- `portfolio_id` is the stable fingerprint of the shared portfolio identifier, BTCUSDT instrument, fixture lineage, and sorted unique member strategy IDs.
- Shared genesis uses `market-os.b4-15.shared-portfolio-genesis@v1`, is immutable, and remains bound to the existing policy fingerprint and synthetic wallet.
- Orders carry a stable `strategy_id`. Only declared members may submit or cancel orders; a member may cancel only its own open order.
- Legacy isolated genesis and order serialization remain recoverable and keep their existing identity rules.

## Reservations and accounting

- Reservations are portfolio-wide and attributed to the owning strategy and order.
- Open sell orders reserve BTC inventory. Open buy orders reserve cash and buy-side inventory capacity.
- Limit buy cash reservation uses limit notional; market buy reservation uses the approved maximum order notional. Shared reservations conservatively round notional and fee upward to the policy price quantum.
- Terminal order state releases its reservation. `inspect("reservations")` exposes per-order attribution and aggregate cash, sell-BTC, and buy-quantity reservations.
- Reservation validation runs during shared recovery: no negative wallet, unknown member, duplicate order identity, cash over-reservation, sell over-reservation, or inventory-cap over-reservation is accepted.

## Ordering, locking, stale writers, conflicts, and retries

- One shared local portfolio has one thread lock and one OS writer lock. Lock acquisition serializes commits; no fairness guarantee is made.
- The append-only ledger sequence and hash chain define committed order. Market fills retain existing acceptance/ledger order.
- A broker instance records the ledger head it loaded. A changed head rejects every subsequent mutation with the existing stale-writer error; the caller must reload before retrying.
- Idempotency remains scoped to portfolio and command kind. Same key and fingerprint returns the recorded result without another effect. A different fingerprint appends a keyless conflict audit event and raises `BrokerRejected`.
- Membership and order-owner failures are typed, append-only rejected outcomes and are idempotent on retry.

## Recovery, replay, checkpoints, and audit

- Recovery reads genesis, policy, ledger, and checkpoint under the same local writer lock used for mutation.
- Each ledger record remains append-only and binds sequence, prior/event fingerprints, resulting snapshot fingerprint, and the resulting snapshot. Recovery restores those verified snapshots and validates shared membership and reservation invariants; it does not claim independent semantic event reduction.
- A checkpoint may identify any verified ledger sequence and is marked `stale_or_missing` when it lags the ledger. It cannot supersede or rewrite the ledger.
- Fresh recovery preserves fingerprints, order/fill state, reservations, risk/approval audit inspection, and replay status.

## Non-goals

Partial fills, expiry, retention/archive, schema transformation, externally authenticated mutation, distributed locks, real data, Binance, credentials, providers, live venues, and production infrastructure remain separate gates.
