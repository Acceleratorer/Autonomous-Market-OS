# B4.20 Read-Only Retention and Archive Preflight

**Status:** Implemented locally for the synthetic, paper-only portfolio store.
**Date:** 2026-10-30

## Purpose

B4.17 sets indefinite source-ledger retention and B4.11 provides a validated
local backup copy. B4.20 makes the current state inspectable before any future
archive or retention decision. It does not change the approved retention
policy or make any store eligible for deletion.

## Contract

`preflight_retention_archive(store_root, portfolio_id, archive_path=None)` is a
read-only Python operation. It validates one recovered portfolio and, when
provided, one local backup archive. The returned versioned report contains:

- the stable portfolio ID, isolated/shared mode, shared-member count, and
  current open-order and cash/inventory reservation totals;
- ledger and checkpoint presence, sizes, SHA-256 fingerprints, record count,
  and the broker's validated recovery/checkpoint status;
- `zero_event_ambiguous=true` when both ledger and checkpoint are absent,
  because the existing format cannot distinguish a newly initialized wallet
  from deleted zero-event history;
- archive status (`not_supplied`, `missing`, `valid`, or `invalid`), archive
  SHA-256, manifest fingerprint, and validated member count when available;
- the current `indefinite-local` source-retention policy and
  `deletion_eligible=false` in every result.

Raw filesystem paths, archive member contents, credentials, and capability
tokens are not returned. Missing, malformed, tampered, symlinked, mismatched,
or changing archive inputs fail closed as an `invalid` archive result. The
archive validator checks the exact allowlisted member set, schema, identity,
manifest fields, member sizes, and streamed SHA-256 fingerprints without
extracting files. It rejects symlink/reparse aliases in every existing path
component, validates the opened regular-file descriptor, applies compressed and
uncompressed size caps, and rechecks the archive identity and digest after
validation so a concurrent replacement is reported as invalid.

## Invariants

- Portfolio recovery uses the existing read-only broker open and writer-lock
  path. A missing lock or changed ledger head is an error; inspection does not
  provision storage or locks.
- Portfolio files and bytes, ledger, checkpoint, writer lock, and archive are
  unchanged by the operation.
- Shared membership and active reservations are reported from the recovered
  broker projection. They do not grant deletion eligibility.
- Archive validation is local, bounded in memory, and uses no network or
  provider. A missing or invalid archive never changes the source portfolio.
- Restore promotion stages and validates a complete portfolio, then publishes
  it with one directory rename; a concurrent destination or publication
  interruption is rejected and never leaves a partial destination.
- No archive listing, pruning, deletion, source-ledger truncation, retention
  override, HTTP route, or mutation CLI route is added.

## Future deletion gate

Any deletion or pruning requires a separate versioned contract and explicit
owner decision covering retention eligibility, active-order/reservation
guards, archive destination and immutable identity, authorization, audit
events, restore/rollback, and interruption recovery. B4.20 supplies evidence
for that decision without assuming its outcome.
